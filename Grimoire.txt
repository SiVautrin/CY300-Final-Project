GRIMOIRE

Open Grimoire.html for illustrated references to the three known spell marks.
The clues below can help you remember how each form feels.
Select the matching ink color before drawing: red for fire, gray for wind,
and brown for earth.

FIRE ATTACK
A sharp, zigzagging mark that changes direction like a flash across the sky.

WIND BLOCK
A smooth, curling mark that circles inward, like a gust winding around itself.

EARTH HEAL
A sturdy mark with four even sides and balanced corners.
